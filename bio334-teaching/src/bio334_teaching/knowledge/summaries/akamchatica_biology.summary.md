# A. kamchatica Biology (Summary)

## Species
- Allotetraploid (2n=4x=32), Brassicaceae
- Hybrid of A. halleri (heavy metal hyperaccumulator) x A. lyrata (non-accumulator)
- Distribution: Japan, Taiwan, Kamchatka, Alaska

## Homoeologs
Duplicated gene copies from the two parental species within the polyploid:
- H-homoeolog: halleri-derived copy
- L-homoeolog: lyrata-derived copy

## HMA4 Gene (Heavy Metal ATPase 4)
- Encodes P-type ATPase for Zn/Cd transport
- A. halleri: 3-4 tandem copies (hyperaccumulation)
- A. lyrata: single copy (no hyperaccumulation)

## Dataset
- 20 accessions from Japan, Alaska, Taiwan
- Source: Paape, Hatakeyama et al. (2016) MBE

## Key Results
| Subgenome | pi |
|-----------|-----|
| halleri HMA4 | 0.000830 (very low) |
| lyrata HMA4 | 0.009133 (~11x higher) |
| D_a (net divergence) | 0.039140 |

## Biological Interpretation
Low pi in halleri-derived HMA4 suggests selective sweep -- positive selection on the functionally critical copy for heavy metal hyperaccumulation.

## Common Mistakes
- Confusing homoeologs with paralogs or orthologs
- Ignoring population structure effects when sampling across geography
